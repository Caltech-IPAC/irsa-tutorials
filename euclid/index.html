Euclid Tutorial Notebooks

Euclid launched in July 2023 with the primary science goals of better understanding the composition and evolution of the dark Universe. It carries two instruments: the VISible instrument (VIS) and the Near-Infrared Spectrometer and Photometer (NISP).

Quick Release 1 (Q1) was released in March 2025 and consists of approximately 35 TB of imaging, spectroscopy, and catalogs, covering four non-contiguous fields totaling 63 square degrees. Data products include MERged mosaics of calibrated and stacked frames; combined infrared spectra (SIR); and catalogs of MER objects, photometric redshifts and classifications (PHZ), and spectroscopic redshifts and line measurements (SPE).

Data Release 1 (DR1) comprises about 1900 square degrees of imaging, spectroscopy, and catalogs from the Euclid Wide Survey and the Euclid Deep Survey, reprocessed with updated pipelines and calibrations, including the Q1 areas. The notebooks below are being updated from Q1 to DR1; the title of each notebook says which release it uses.

Data products include:

  • MER (merged) mosaic images of calibrated and stacked frames;

  • SIR combined infrared spectra;

  • Catalogs of MER objects, photometric redshifts and classifications (PHZ), and spectroscopic redshifts and line measurements (SPE);

  • Merged Objects Catalog (created by IRSA) containing the MER, PHZ, and SPE catalogs in a single HATS Catalog.

MER Image Mosaics →

Search the DR1 wide and deep MER mosaics, identify the official wide survey mosaic, make multiwavelength cutouts, compare wide and deep images, and subtract the background and extract sources.

SIR 1D Spectra →

Search for the Euclid DR1 SIR spectra of a galaxy, pick the best red and blue grism spectra, and plot them with their emission lines.

MER Catalogs →

Choose among the DR1 MER catalog tables, clean a sample with the quality columns, select stars, plot their SEDs and VIS cutouts, and make a color-color diagram.

PHZ Catalogs →

Join the DR1 PHZ and MER catalogs to select galaxies with good photometric redshifts in a region, overlay them on a MER mosaic cutout, and compare the photometric redshift of one of them with its SIR spectrum and spectroscopic redshift.

SPE Catalogs →

Join the DR1 SPE tables with the MER and PHZ catalogs, filter them for galaxies with a strong H-alpha line following the DR1 SPE paper, see what each criterion removes, and plot the spectra and line measurements of galaxies that pass or fail the criteria.

DR1 HATS Catalogs: Introduction →

Learn which Euclid DR1 catalogs are available in HATS format and how they are organized, then perform a basic query and a cone search.

Merged Objects Catalog: Introduction →

Understand the content and format of the Euclid Q1 Merged Objects HATS Catalog, then perform a basic query.

Merged Objects Catalog: Magnitudes →

Review the types of flux measurements available, load template-fit and aperture magnitudes, and plot distributions and comparisons for different object types.

Euclid Cloud Access →

Browse the on-cloud copy of DR1, then efficiently retrieve a MER mosaic cutout and a SIR spectrum.

Galaxy Clusters →

Use Euclid Q1 data to detect and validate a galaxy cluster.

Early Release Observations (ERO) - Star Clusters →

Create multi-wavelength ERO image cutouts of a globular cluster, extract sources, and measure photometry. Match Gaia sources with Euclid ERO catalogs, then visualize with Firefly.
